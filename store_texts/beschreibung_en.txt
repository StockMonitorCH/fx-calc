FX Calc combines a calculator, currency converter and finance calculator in one lean app. Free, no ads and no sign-up.

CURRENCY CONVERTER
• Live exchange rates from Yahoo Finance
• More than 20 currencies, including CHF, EUR, USD, GBP and JPY
• Cryptocurrencies: Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP and Solana
• Precious metals and commodities: gold, silver, platinum, palladium and copper
• Quick picks for the most common currency pairs
• Swap currencies with one tap

CALCULATOR
• Brackets, percent, square root and square
• Thousands separators based on your region settings
• Larger keys if you prefer
• Use results directly in the currency converter and vice versa

SAVINGS CALCULATOR
• Compound interest with initial capital, duration and interest rate
• Yearly deposits (savings plan) or withdrawals (withdrawal plan)
• Overview of total paid in and interest earned
• For withdrawals: how long the capital lasts

FINANCE CALCULATOR
• Time value of money (TVM): solve for periods, rate, present value, payment or future value
• Amortization: interest and principal for any range of periods
• Cash flow analysis with net present value (NPV) and internal rate of return (IRR)
• Interest conversion between nominal and effective rate
• Break-even analysis

PRIVACY
FX Calc does not store any personal data. Only the selected currency pairs are requested from Yahoo Finance to get exchange rates.
